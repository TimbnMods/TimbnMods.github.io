# Helpers

Timbn Core includes several helper methods to make modding easier.

- [TimbnConfig](timbn-config.md)
  Binding a slider or a hidden setting, moving a renamed entry's value, and reloading config files.

- [TimbnInput](timbn-input.md)
  When a mod's hotkeys should and should not fire.

- [TimbnUI](timbn-ui.md)
  The game's windows, screen fades, and menus a mod draws.

- [TimbnItems](timbn-items.md)
  Counting, giving, taking, and dropping items.

- [TimbnPlayer](timbn-player.md)
  Where the player is, what they have, and moving them.

- [TimbnClock](timbn-clock.md)
  The calendar and the time of day.

- [TimbnWorld](timbn-world.md)
  Finding world objects and ground, and reading the graveyard, town and weather.

- [TimbnZombies](timbn-zombies.md)
  Finding zombies and changing how they look.
